Nigeria Fuel Prices API

Free, static JSON. State-by-state petrol, diesel, kerosene and cooking gas prices from the National Bureau of Statistics.

Loading latest figures…

These are monthly state averages published by NBS a few weeks after each month ends. They are a baseline, not live pump prices. Every response carries the period it describes.

Endpoints

/v1/index.jsonCoverage and the list of endpoints
/v1/national.jsonNational average, highest and lowest states, zone averages
/v1/states.jsonAll 36 states + FCT with their latest price
/v1/prices/{state}.jsonOne state: latest figure, rank, change, full history
/v1/fuels/{fuel}/latest.jsonEvery state for the latest month (pms, ago, dpk or lpg)
/v1/fuels/{fuel}/history.jsonFull monthly series, also as CSV
/v1/news.jsonPrices stated in recent news headlines. Leads, not measurements; never mixed into the NBS figures
/v1/community.jsonPump prices reported through the Telegram bot in the last 72 hours (appears once the bot is connected)
/v1/meta/sources.jsonThe NBS workbooks behind the numbers
/v1/meta/issues.jsonProblems found in the source files, and what was left out

Example

const r = await fetch("v1/prices/lagos.json");
const { fuels } = await r.json();
console.log(fuels.pms.latest.price, fuels.pms.latest.period);

State slugs are lowercase with hyphens: akwa-ibom, cross-river, fct (also abuja).
